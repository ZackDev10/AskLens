Atlas Home Store - Returns and Refunds Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The 30 days start on the day the package is delivered, not the day you place the order.

2. Condition of the item
Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories and manuals. Items that show signs of use, damage caused by the customer, or missing parts may be refused or refunded only partially.

3. Items that cannot be returned
Gift cards are not refundable. Personalised or engraved products cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective. Opened hygiene products, such as earphone tips, cannot be returned.

4. How to start a return
Send your order number and the reason for the return to returns@atlashome.example. Our team replies within 2 business days with a return label or the address of the nearest drop-off point. Do not ship the item before you receive this confirmation.

5. Return shipping costs
If the item is defective or we sent the wrong product, we pay for return shipping. For any other reason, the customer pays the return shipping cost.

6. Refunds
Once we receive and inspect the item, the refund is issued to the original payment method within 5 business days. For cash on delivery orders, the refund is sent by bank transfer, so we will ask for your bank details by email.

7. Exchanges
If you want a different size or colour, place a new order and return the original item. This is the fastest way to make sure the item you want is still in stock.
